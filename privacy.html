Chunks Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Chunks is a homework, chore and schedule organizer for kids. We built it so that it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Chunks does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-up.

Where your information lives

The tasks, chunks, schedule items and first name you type into Chunks are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this information. If you use your device's own backup (such as iCloud Backup), that copy is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

No ads, tracking or analytics

Chunks contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking tools, no in-app purchases and no links that leave the app. The app does not connect to the internet.

Children's privacy

Chunks is designed for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there is no information for parents to review or delete. This is consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store rules for the Kids Category.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. We will not start collecting information without updating this policy first.
