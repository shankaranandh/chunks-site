Chunks

Big jobs, small steps. Chunks helps kids organize homework, chores and their weekly schedule.

Help

Split a task into chunks: open a task and type a step under "Chunks", then tap +. Finishing the last chunk finishes the task.

Add something to the weekly schedule: go to the Schedule tab, tap +, pick the days and times.

Delete something: swipe left on it.
